AeonFlux Android Client

Industrial Infrastructure & News Reader Client Platform

Version 1.0.0 (Build 20260809)
Application Overview

AeonFlux is a high-performance, reactive Android client featuring offline Room database synchronization, OPML feed ingestion, background WorkManager RSS fetching, embedded/private browser engines, and dynamic media playback with on-device Vosk speech-to-text karaoke transcription.
